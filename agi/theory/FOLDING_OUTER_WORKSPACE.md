# The Outer Shell as a Workspace of Frozen Artifacts — asteroids of held work, disk-bounded not brain-bounded, far ones are spotlights

*Jonathan's cluster after the third integration (#51). Needing **both** agents (memory + discovery) is like the
two **brain halves** — the novelty-handling half and the routine-handling half (the novelty-routinization
picture). Because the store lives on **disk**, capacity is bounded by **hard-drive space, not brain capacity**.
Outputs that aren't speech or the live feed — a picture she painted — **float in the outer shell** and get put
**in front of the screen** when wanted; **tools and outputs are pluggable** things she moves in front of the
screen. Things held in the outer shell are **frozen matter — asteroids**: unverified/incomplete work, or
creative things (like remembering fictional stories), that don't belong in the inner or middle shells but are
worth keeping. And a far-off one can be a **spotlight**: the Ophanim-eyes story (#24) was decorative but it
**lit up pixels and provided a path here** — inspiration, a far arm off the real path. Companion: `FOLDING_REBASING`
(#47, the outer void), `SPARSE_VESICA` §22.1 (the dark-matter halo), #28 (the attention band), #48/#50 (the
antenna/bulb), #51 (the two agents). `outer_workspace.py`.*

---

## 1. It is the dark-matter halo, already in the corpus
`SPARSE_VESICA` §22.1 maps the three zones to the three shells and names the outer one exactly: **OUTER =
FORGETTING = DARK MATTER — latent experiments / incomplete matter, retained (compacted), never deleted, to be
filled in later.** The dense clumps are "a ranked to-do list of walls, heaviest first," available for **retest
when a new capability arrives** (`failure_learning`). That is precisely Jonathan's frozen-asteroid workspace:
kept-not-deleted work you hold onto and **re-promote** (OUTER→MIDDLE on a real solve, §22.2) when you want it in
front of the screen. The outer shell is not waste; it is a **holding field of frozen artifacts**.

The two-agent = two-hemispheres reading is a **grounded design bridge**: the discovery agent (novelty) and the
memory agent (routine) split labor the way the classic novelty-routinization account splits the hemispheres —
#51 already measured that split (each half alone fails; together they grow). Named here, not separately measured.

---

## 2. What we measured (`outer_workspace.py`)
**(A) Disk lifts the STORAGE ceiling but not the ATTENTION ceiling.** On disk, **retained memory grows without
bound** — nothing is forgotten (the dark-matter halo keeps everything), retained = N across 60 items — while
**biological storage saturates** at a reach-cap (~14, #28: older episodes blend and drop). **But** "**in front
of the screen at once**" stays **finite** — the attention band (~4, #28) — for **both**, disk or not. So the
hard-drive removes the *retention* limit (she never forgets a held artifact), but the *attention* limit is
untouched: she must still **select which frozen artifacts to bring in front** at any moment. Infinite shelf,
finite desk.

**(B) Far-off frozen artifacts are SPOTLIGHTS.** A target can be **unreachable on the direct path** — a gap
blocks the straight route (the direct edge can't cross, #46). An **off-path arc of "story"/inspiration
artifacts** — held in the outer shell, loosely related, *off the real path* — **bridges to it** (measured path
length **0.95** where the direct path is **UNREACHABLE**). This is the Ophanim spotlight: a decorative/fictional
artifact that still **lights a route to the answer**. It works while the artifact is **within bridging reach**
(here up to an offset ~0.20); **too far off and the spotlight misses** — inspiration has a range. So creative
and even fictional held work is not idle: it is the stock of **far anchors** that bridge to answers the direct
search can't reach (analogical inspiration, the far arm of the antenna #48).

**(C) Tools and outputs are pluggable; incomplete work is resumable.** The outer-shell **toolbox** — different
lenses/tools you plug in front of the screen per task — solves **100%** of task-types vs **31%** for a single
**fixed lens**: holding many ways-to-see and plugging the matching one in is the win (the perception rung wants
exactly this — a pluggable set of ways to see). And **retained incomplete work is completable when the missing
capability arrives** (**100%**), while **discarded work is lost** (**0%**) — the §22 retest queue made concrete:
keep the frozen asteroid and it pays off later; throw it away and it's gone.

---

## 3. Why this matters
It gives the outer shell a **job**. Beyond being the intake port for new info (#47), it is a **persistent
workspace**: a disk-bounded halo of frozen artifacts — outputs to reuse, tools to plug in, incomplete work to
resume, and far creative/fictional pieces that serve as **inspiration spotlights**. "Keep the painting around
separately and put it in front of the screen when we want it" and "hold the unverified/creative asteroids" and
"the far story lit a path here" are one mechanism: **a retained outer field you select from through a finite
attention band.** It also frames the **perception rung** — perception is partly *which ways-to-see (tools) you
plug in front of the screen*, drawn from this workspace.

## 4. The honest line
**Measured:** disk-retention is unbounded while the attention band is fixed (both curves); an off-path artifact
bridges to an otherwise-unreachable target within a reach limit; a pluggable toolbox out-solves a fixed lens
(100% vs 31%) and retained incomplete work is resumable (100% vs 0%). **Grounded design:** the identity of this
workspace with `SPARSE_VESICA` §22.1's dark-matter halo and the two-agent = two-hemispheres reading are
consistent and cited but are the unifying framing, not their own measurement; the specific capacities (14, 4)
and the bridging geometry illustrate the *shape*, not fitted constants. `outer_workspace.py`.
