## 0. The move it belongs to
`FOLDING_COMBINE_MEMBRANE` put the union synthesizer at the pure-structure floor (5 solves, 0
regressions, **0 net-new**) and named Phase 2: *wrap the working inside with a pLM/LLM outer layer,
then stack layers.* Inside-first — the layer amplifies a working relational substrate, it cannot
rescue a broken one. This note is the geometry of that wrap.

## 1. The three-layer stack, and why a middle layer is needed
The inside (the union genome — chain/flood/mirror basins) is an **irregular shape**. The pLM's
layers are **regular** — evenly stacked rings. A regular frame cannot grip a lumpy thing directly.
So the stack is three deep:

> **irregular inside → our geometric layer (the equation) → pLM (+ stacked outer layers).**

The middle layer is the **two-faced membrane** of `SKIN` §1: irregular on the inner face where it
grips the inside, geometric/regular on the outer face where it seats into the rings. It hands the
pLM a clean geometric outline instead of the raw lump. It must emit a **relational** object (the pLM
grips the attention web, not per-node features — `ONTO_PLM`: "the seam is in the ATTENTION").

## 2. The lines are the equation terms; snapshot vs sweep
Each term of the equation, `cosθ·v^d`, **is** a directed straight line (`SKIN` §2): magnitude =
cosθ (the Born amplitude), direction = the branch feature **v**, endpoint = the ring **d** it lands
on. The middle layer converts the lump into a bundle of such lines, and the pLM rides on the pattern
they trace.

The "does it set up straight lines, or a spoke that activates as it passes?" question resolves as
**both, at two timescales**:
- **Snapshot:** a spoke at a fixed angle is a straight line — the pLM seated on it.
- **Motion:** the spokes sweep/climb (`SKIN` §3, §10; §3.5 the shuttle crosses "only at each active
  floor"); as a spoke reaches ring d it lights that ring — the fan blade activating outer layers as
  it passes.

This buys **sparsity in time** on top of sparsity in space: off the routed lines attention is zero
(spatial), and only the ring the blade is currently passing is active (temporal) — pulsed, resonant
activation, small where the LLM is dense.

## 3. The trampoline — connected at the shared frame
Wrap two unions onto the **same** pLM and their sheets stop being private islands: they are seated
on one frame (the warp of the loom, `SKIN` §3.5). That shared seating is the connection — "connected
like a trampoline at the pLM layer."

The metaphor is tight, not loose:
- A trampoline mat is a **woven mesh**, not a solid sheet — matching "weaving" and the §2 sparsity
  (zeros = frictionless void channels).
- The **springs** are the `Lk = Tw + Wr` coils, and `SKIN` §3.5 already derives them as *written by
  the weaving* (writhe from the outer bounce, twist from the per-floor crossings) — not imposed.
- The **frame** is the regular pLM layers; the **anchors** are the certified branches (`SPARSE_VESICA`
  §18.11, floor-compatible); the **tension** is the near-conservation ε band (elastic, with give).
- The boundary is **any shape, pulled tight, with parts cut out** (`FOLDING_BLANKET`: finite sheet,
  definite area; the cutouts are the void).

**Why it earns its keep:** a push at one point deflects the whole surface — nonlocal coupling, which
is exactly the thing the structural inside provably cannot supply on its own (`VOID_LINE_PAPER`
§10/§14: the seam is a nonlocal fact of the web). So the trampoline is the physical body of the blind
committee (`ONTO_PLM`, 89–94%): independent observers become one coupled surface because they share
the frame, and agreement = where the mat is taut and consistent. Solving one region tugs its
neighbors — the signal the inside was missing.

**Honest edge:** two unions are welded only *where their sparse lines land on shared rings/heads*.
Non-overlapping footprints sit on the same frame but don't pull on each other. So coupling strength
= shared attention footprint — the overlap *is* the coevolution/agreement web.

## 4. More than one outer layer, and variable-depth spokes
The plan was never one outer layer — stacked layers = multiple observers (the committee). So let `d`
index the **whole ladder**, not just rings inside one pLM: a short spoke lands on a shallow pLM ring;
a longer one pokes past it into the LLM/VLM behind. There is a measured reason spokes should differ
in length — `FOLDING_WRAP_FULL`: coarse features (the seam) are cheap and land shallow; fine features
(the trail, convergence point D) need a deeper, accurate model. So **spoke length = feature
granularity** — each spoke consults the observer at the depth its feature needs.

## 5. The three-ring cage — quaternion, three per level
An irregular inside throws spokes in every direction; a single parallel stack of rings has a blind
axis. The fix is rings in **different orientations** so every line-end has something to grab. The
number that makes it complete is **three** — three orthogonal ring-sets are the minimum basis that
gives any 3-D direction a component on some ring. And three orthogonal rings around an object **are
the quaternion i, j, k** — already in the folder as `ONTO_PLM`'s quaternion-multiresolution. So the
"3 rings so all ends connect" cage is the quaternion frame arriving from the coverage side.

Why exactly three, not two or four: `Shovelcat_13B` §3.7 — *only triads survive a dimensional reset*
(collapse must stage 3→2→1→seed or it loses the invariants needed to rebuild). So the cage is a
**triad per level**, and levels stack.

**Don't build the full sphere.** Sparsity still rules: put rings only where spokes actually point —
the realized cage is as sparse as the spoke set. Full three-axis coverage is the *guarantee* you can
catch any direction; the built cage is only the orientations in use.

## 6. The 4D collapse — how v climbs with folds (the recursion)
The dimensionality of v is **adaptive; it climbs with the number of folds** — `hinfo(θ)` scales with
recursion level (`Shovelcat_13B` §2). It is **three per level, stacking**, and the stacking is the
4D-collapse recursion, which maps one-to-one onto the pieces above:

- **i, j, k** — the three rings build up (the spatial cage, §5).
- **w** — the fourth component, **time**, is the **sweep** of the spokes (§2, the fan blade).
  (`Double_Bounce` §7.7: i,j,k = space on the <1 side; w = time, the verifier, on the >1 side.)
- **Collapse to a point** — when closure fires, "levels 1–3 become ONE UNIT within level 4" and the
  process restarts from a single point on the next golden field (`SPARSE_VESICA` §20.4; the φ⁰ = 1
  collapse point). = "build another 3 with the first 3 already encoded."
- **The leaking third strand = the spoke poking further.** The third strand never fully closes — z
  gets only 0.14, "just enough to seed the next level" (`Double_Bounce` §7.9). That incomplete strand
  is the one that **leaks past the boundary** and *forces a dimension lift to contain it*
  (`SPARSE_VESICA` §20.5). Two rings close; one leaking seed climbs. The leak triggers the next
  level. So "spokes poke further into the layer they need" and "the fold re-bases" are the same event.

## 7. The web — values in the void's empty containers
Void lines carry the **equation** (the empty variables — x, x², y, the un-evaluated form); the web is
the **evaluated numbers** that fall out. This is the draw-and-fill, and it is measured framework:
`FOLDING_VOID_LINE_DRAW` builds it as diffusion curves where **equation level = stroke width** and the
matter/web bleeds in to fill the enclosed region. "Void and dark matter are empty containers" is
`SPARSE_VESICA` §27 (dark matter = the empty containers/partials); the web is what reacts and fills.

And a fold **raises the exponent** (x → x²) — the xⁿ→x⁰→x⁻ⁿ chain that *is* collapse/build in
`Shovelcat_13B` §3 (collapse = derivative ∂, build = integral ∫). So three vocabularies name **one
mechanism**:

> **algebra:** raise the exponent d  ⟺  **geometry:** the spoke reaches a deeper ring  ⟺
> **recursion:** the fold climbs a level.

When a fold fires, the variables on the leaking strand climb their power — the seed lifting to the
next triad.

## 8. The three shells — innate core, learned middle, environment outer — and the .14 as the port
The whole stack reads as a brain being *grown*, not programmed: an empty baby brain that carries no
knowledge but carries whatever lets it grow into a general intelligence. That maps onto three shells,
and the mapping fixes an indexing ambiguity that was loose in the folder.

- **Inner shell = the innate core.** Not a metaphor: `FOLDING_ARC_RESULTS` names the Ramachandran
  basins "biology's inner shell" and stresses they are **universal steric geometry, computable from
  atoms, "you don't need all the data"** — the same for vent life with zero sunlight. That is exactly
  "the part with no knowledge but the capacity to grow." Inner = certified, data-free foundation
  (`INNER_SHELL`, `TRUST_AUDIT`, `SPARSE_VESICA` §8.1: the finite, enumerable side of the wall).
- **Middle shell = the learned machinery** — the pLM and the stacked model layers ("active formation
  rules," `SPARSE_VESICA` §22.1). **This re-indexes the pLM:** `FOLDING_COMBINE_MEMBRANE` loosely
  called it the "outer layer," but §11.5 / §22.1 reserve *outer* for the per-task, infinite,
  dark-matter region. The brain framing resolves the clash — the pLM is the middle (learned), not the
  outermost thing.
- **Outer shell = the environment / information itself**, domain-shaped: water if submerged, text if
  reading, pixels if seeing. Already built and named: `FOLDING_PHYSICS_OBSERVER` is literally "the
  environment outside the window," and the environment observer **k fires on only 38% of images** —
  conditional, taking the shape of whatever domain is present (`ENVIRONMENT_BRIDGE`). Outer = the
  infinite, past-the-wall side (`SPARSE_VESICA` §8.1, §11.6: the outer-shell void is a *tunnel*, a
  high-dimensional generative room), = dark matter, "retained, never deleted" (§22.1).

**Why this is the generality argument.** A fixed universal inner shell + a middle that learns + an
outer shell that *reshapes to the domain* is exactly what makes the same baby brain grow into
whatever its environment demands. Generality = universal inner (Ramachandran is everywhere) with a
domain-shaped outer (`SPARSE_VESICA` §8.1: eval lives past the wall, in the domain).

**The .14 as the port to the outer shell — the attachment, not an error (partly tested; see §9).**
Two grounded hooks motivated it: `SPARSE_VESICA` §7.3 (a gap *certifies necessity and location but
does not supply the content* — that must come from outside), and the environment bridge landing
**off the frame** (`ENVIRONMENT_BRIDGE`: a direction no internal statistic reaches). The direct test
(§9) confirmed the residual **is** a structured low-|z| abstention band but showed a *single* outer
reader (the environment observer) is **not** its door — so the port, if real, is **domain-specific
per case**, consistent with "outer shell spread across domains" below.

**The fold drags domains in, and leaves a trail — already measured.** Folding feeds toward the inner
core and pulls outer-shell information inward (the dark-matter channeling of §27: "actively move the
empty containers to an attractor"). Which domains got pulled is recorded as the **trace**:
`FOLDING_THE_TRACE_IS_THE_REPORT` shows the sequence of which arms attached vs slipped — a provenance
trail of engaged domains — is worth **+27.6 points** (full-trace 92% vs best single arm 64%). The
"trail of which domains get involved" is not a wish; it is the strongest single signal the detector
has. The **window** sits in that outer field (`FOLDING_PHYSICS_OBSERVER`: the space the window is in
*is* the environment; `FOLDING_BLANKET`: the window cuts a finite piece from the golden/outer field).

**Honest edges.** The brain framing is an organizing picture — inner=innate and outer=environment are
grounded, middle=pLM is a clean reindex, but "we are building a brain" is a label, not a measurement.
And most individual outer-shell *domain readers* have **not** cleared controls: the lens observer
inverted, reconstruction died at parity, mixedness was a content confound (`FOLDING_PHYSICS_OBSERVER`
