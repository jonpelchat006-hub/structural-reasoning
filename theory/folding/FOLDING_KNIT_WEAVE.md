# Knit AND Weave — the chain threads its own void line (the needle eye)

*Companion to `FOLDING_CLEANUP.md`, `FOLDING_SALTS.md`. Jonathan's synthesis: not knit *or* woven but **both** — at the large scale woven (a partition), but the single chain is *knitted* through it (a string through a needle eye, both sides pulled through). Tested by counting backbone crossings of the cutting plane; `thread_test.py`.*

## Result — it is BOTH, exactly as predicted (CONFIRMED)
Every protein has one dominant two-domain seam (woven at the large scale) AND the single chain threads back through it 1–7 times: 1CLL 1 (clean weave), 2LZM 1, 3PGK 2, 8TLN 2, 1PHP 2, 1GD1 5, 4AKE 5, **6LDH 7** (heavily knitted). The large scale is a weave; the chain is knitted through it — both, at different scales, from the same strand. Calmodulin the floor again (1 pass).

## Why it closes the arc
Knit-vs-woven are not alternatives — the same chain at two scales: **substance** (the ~15% woven core where A and B interpenetrate) is produced by **topology** (the single chain threading back through its own seam). So the irreducibly-both core is one thread knitted through the eye, which from a windowed view looks woven. The threading count is a clean per-protein "knittedness" score (1 to 7). Doesn't buy prediction — still needs the structure to count crossings (the wall stands; we now know its knit).
