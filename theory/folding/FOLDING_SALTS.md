# The Salts — is the woven core a salt crystal? is there a voltage across the cut?

*Companion to `FOLDING_CLEANUP.md`, `FOLDING_THE_KNIFE.md` R4, `FOLDING_TWO_WALLS.md`. Jonathan: maybe the uncuttable core is like a salt crystal, unique per protein, an internal salt distinct from solvent salts — and being charge-bearing it sets up a voltage that could move energy. Tested on 6 proteins; `salts.py`.*

## Results
1. **NOT a salt crystal: shape holds the core (bounds the idea).** Charged-residue fraction in the woven core 7–36% — no consistent enrichment. What holds it is **shape complementarity** (snug negative molds), not charge — an interlocked shape lock.
2. **But buried salt bridges DO cross the cut, internal.** Of charged contacts spanning the cut, ~50–100% pair as salt bridges (opposite-sign, <4.5 Å) at ~0.7–0.9× average charged-residue exposure — partially buried, internal rivets (distinct from fully-solvated bulk-water salts). Rivets, not the wall.
3. **The two faces ARE charge-separated: a static voltage (real, small).** In 4/6 proteins the two faces carry opposite net charge (6LDH +3/−2, 1GD1 +2/−2) — a genuine standing dipole across the void line. Real, but small (1–3 net charges) and static — not a current.
4. **Unique to each protein (CONFIRMED)** — the specific D/E/K/R placements are sequence-specific.

## Honest physics on "move energy"
A static charge separation is a potential difference, but a standing dipole doesn't move energy — transduction needs a dynamic element, a gradient source, and a channel/circuit (a membrane); a soluble interface has none of the last two, so it is **not a battery**. But the components are present: the gate brick (ADK salt bridges form/break reversibly, 77/54 — a salt bridge as a switch); ADK is a real energy enzyme. This is the interlock of the two walls — the carbon/shape wall (holds the core) meets the ionic/phosphate energy wall (the salt bridges + charge separation) exactly at the void line. The seam is where structure and energy touch.
