# FOLDING — the honest map (what's load-bearing, what's analogy, what's open)

*A consolidation across the whole folding thread. Four buckets: **CONFIRMED** (measured, robust enough to build on), **ESTABLISHED SCIENCE** (real, imported correctly, not our measurement), **ANALOGY/MAPPING** (real in one domain, carried to another — scaffolding, not proof), **OPEN / CONTESTED**, and **NEGATIVES** (tested and refuted — do not relitigate). Standing caveat: the structural tests run on a small curated set (~8 two-domain enzymes) with Fiedler-bisection seams as a proxy for the min-cut; directions are clean but samples are small.*

## ✓ CONFIRMED — the floors you can build on

**Geometry of the void line**
- **Void line = the rotation axle.** ADK's domains turn ~45° about an axis **2–5 Å from the seam**. The strongest single result — the seam literally is the hinge.
- **Block-and-rotate (induced fit).** Pin the CORE (1.3 Å) and the LID swings **14 Å**, NMP 10 Å, to close on the ligand. "Reading = the closure event," not a scan.
- **The web moves.** 24% of contacts form/break open→closed (matches the gate brick 77/54).
- **Matching slot / complementarity.** 81% of cross-seam charged contacts are opposite-sign at rivet distance, organized beyond chance (z=2.70). Small n.
- **The C-pixel darkroom.** The woven-core residues are more buried than average (p<0.001), 28% fully desolvated.
- Prior thread: wire metered exactly (r=0.87); strands unbundle to 68% coil and clump at the ligand; ligand on the void line reached from both borders; chain threads the seam 1–7×; faces charge-separated into a small static voltage; ~15% irreducibly-both woven core.

**Motion, continuity, observers**
- **Continuity law.** Signed void-line crossings cancel to **|net| ≤ 1, 8/8 proteins**, from **every** viewing angle. Continuity verifying itself — the return leg is free.
- **The ±1 is a height**, view-dependent (vanishes from ~35–40% of angles) while the net-≤1 invariant is angle-proof.
- **Committee recovers the height.** 3 orthogonal observers catch large offsets **100%** of the time; can't invent tiny ones. Agreement = invariant, disagreement = depth (stereo vision).
- **Devil's-staircase zoom.** Finer resolution reveals more crossings, continuity holds at every scale, finite floor = residue count.
- **Quaternion committee.** 1 invariant + 3 orthogonal observers = 1 real + i/j/k.

## ✗ NEGATIVES — tested and refuted (settled)
- "Read one side at a time" (antiparallel register): NO — 43% antiparallel = ordinary packing.
- "Read one side" (exposure asymmetry): NO — partner exposures positively correlated. The register is symmetric.
- "Light from 3 directions": NO — a seam core is approached from **2** walls.
- window→seam is NOT readable from the local alphabet (the wall); the void line does NOT predict the active site better than "guess the center."
