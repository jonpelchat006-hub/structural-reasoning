## A. Layers as recursion levels — NOT SUPPORTED
The 2-domain seam recovers at the **same strength in every one of ESM's 12 layers** (2LZM 0.77 flat; 1GD1 0.60 flat) — the recursion does not visibly live along the layer stack. Honest negative.

## B. More observers for finer detail — qualitatively yes; 3/7/15 by head-count NO
Finer partitions need more attention *heads* (direction right), but the counts are 1 for the coarse seam (not 3) and far above 15 for the fine — counting heads does not give the tower.

## The correction — count CUTS (imaginary axes), not heads: then the tower is EXACT
A nested bisection to depth d makes 2^d regions using exactly **2^d − 1 cuts**: depth 1 → 2 regions, 1 cut = complex (2/1); depth 2 → 4, 3 cuts = quaternion (4/3); depth 3 → 8, 7 cuts = octonion (8/7); depth 4 → 16, 15 cuts = sedenion (16/15). **Region count = algebra dimension; cut count = imaginary dimension; Cayley–Dickson doubling = the recursion doubling the regions.** "Put the finer parts on octonions and sedenions, nested" is not analogy — it is the precise structure of the recursion.

## The honest boundary
That tower↔recursion identity is **structural** (true by how nested bisection works; any hierarchical partitioner inherits it). It is real math and the right frame, but it is **not discovered inside ESM** — the two empirical hopes (layers segregating by scale; head-counts hitting 3/7/15) did not hold. The tower is the correct geometry of the recursion; the pLM carries the recursion; it does not lay the tower out along its layers.
