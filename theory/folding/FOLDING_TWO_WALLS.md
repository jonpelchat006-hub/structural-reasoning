# Two Walls — the carbon scaffold and the phosphate energy system

*Companion to `RIP_TEST_RESULTS.md`, `FOLDING_RIP_ROADMAP.md`. Jonathan's claim: there are **two universal walls** for life — **carbon** (structure) and **ATP/phosphate** (energy) — and Earth life is a recursively-evolved slice biased toward carbon. Tested at the element level; `comp.py`, `atpase.py`, `geo.py`.*

## Result 1 — the two walls are separable at the element level (CONFIRMED)
Protein scaffold (10 structures): **63% C, 19% O, 17% N, 0.7% S, 0% P** — the structural wall is carbon and **phosphorus-free**. Adenylate kinase: protein 0 P; the bound ATP-analog carries **all 11 P atoms** at one site. Two walls: structure = carbon world (proteins); energy/information = phosphorus world (ATP, DNA/RNA backbone).

## Result 2 — life is a biased slice, tilted hard toward carbon (CONFIRMED)
Body-vs-crust enrichment: **C ~925×, N ~1684×** (scaffold, grabbed far beyond abundance); P ~10×, S ~9×, H ~68× (functional); **Si ~1/9000×, Al ~1/90000×, Fe ~1/900×** (rock-forming elements rejected — Si is 28% of the crust, life wants almost none). Life is a narrow slice on the carbon line, not a neutral sample.

## Result 3 — the ATP machine is a 3-fold rotary motor (CONFIRMED)
The F1-ATPase (1BMF): **3 catalytic β "windows" at 120°** (measured 119–127°) around one central γ pivot; the three are in **3 different states** (empty/loose/tight, Boyer's binding-change: RMSDs 3.9/3.8/0.6 Å) while the scaffold α ring is nearly symmetric (~1.2 Å). The γ pivot is **off-axis/asymmetric** — a symmetric frame + asymmetric shaft pushes each window into a different state. The carbon-protein rotor is the machine that *makes* the ATP — wall one pivots on wall two to manufacture it.

### Result 3b — the window geometry, read off the structure
The window is a **WEDGE** (~2–8 Å at the pivot tip to ~58 Å at the rim, ~7×), nucleotides on a **constant-radius ring r ≈ 30 Å** (catalytic + noncatalytic site per sector), one catalytic site **empty** (βE), the γ shaft an off-center asymmetric lump — the motor's lean.

## Open (astrobiology)
Whether carbon+ATP is necessary or contingent — whether a different tilt of the shell could run life on other elements — is unobserved and unknown.
