## "Light from 3 directions?" — NO, it is 2, and it tracks the ligand
Clustering contact directions around seam-bound cofactors: **1AKE (AP5A) 2 directions, 1GD1 (NAD) 2** (the two domain walls); 2CTS (CoA) 4 — but CoA is an extended ligand gripped along its length. The seam is fundamentally **two-walled**; the number of approach directions is set by the shape of what's read, not a constant 3. A genuine "third" is the **opening** (the cleft mouth), a gap, not a third wall.

## The rest
- **D — correct and important:** A/B = the two domains, C = their mix, **D = the cofactor** (retinal/NAD/ATP/metal) — the non-protein guest that makes a pocket a *reader* instead of a hole. Affirmed.
- **Multiple cores at segment ends — half true:** the crossings converge on **one** core (single active site), not a row of readers.
- **Directional flow — real:** the seam is lopsided (strand counts ~6:1) with asymmetric pile-up toward one domain.
- **"Faces hand off to make agreement" = the matching-slot complementarity** (81% opposite-sign at rivet distance) — the same "convergence = agreement" principle as the ARC thread. Corrected picture: a two-walled seam holding a D-class cofactor at crossings that converge on one core, directional flow, faces that agree by complementarity — everything stands except the "3 directions" (it's 2 walls + a door).
