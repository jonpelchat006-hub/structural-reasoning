# Parameters the theory must fix — with the constraints the runs impose

Each row: what the parameter is, what the folder measured, and the constraint that follows. Values in the "used" column are what produced the results in THETA_FORMD_RESULTS.md; they are choices, not derivations.

| Parameter | Published / used | What the runs showed | Constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Magnitude-plane weight (w_E p_E, form of the factor) | Table 1: 0.4 under a square root (max f_E ~ 1.9 at r_E = 150). Used: 0.96, no root. **Leading candidate (17 Sep): the degeneracy weight w = 0.4 + 0.56 \|s*\|, measured from the strain frame — 0.4 when the three principal lines are distinct, 0.96 when two merge; 0.8–0.95 at the load, 0.55–0.70 in the bulk; better locator than fixed 0.7, less volume than fixed 0.96 (LINES addendum). Carried into θ_s as the amplitude (per cell or per Γ bubble, WEIGHT_RINGS96 addendum) it shrinks Γ 4–8× and weakens the sink (−4.78 → −1.2 % at ν=.01) because it is ≤ 0.96 everywhere — its value is as a locator, not an amplifier; keep 0.96 in θ_s until a matched-volume comparison is run.** | 0.4: pi unreachable from the kinetic plane in any run; 0.96: static p99 of theta_s is 3.7 already at E0 = .15, so the interface forms at low load too and dilutes at high Re. Two-plane product with Table-1 weights: 2.44 -> 2.78 (E0 .15 -> .6); with 0.96: 5.6 -> 6.9. | pi should be reached only when the load is real. On the tube family that means theta_s crosses pi between E0 = .15 (r_E ~ 16) and E0 = .6 (r_E ~ 24): with theta_s = 1 + w ln r_E that is w between (pi-1)/ln 24 = 0.67 and (pi-1)/ln 16 = 0.77. A weight near 0.7 makes pi require the strong case on this family; whether that transfers to other flows is exactly what the external test is for. Carried-weight test (17 Sep): per-cell w = 0.4 + 0.56 A (A = ring share of frame rotation) is too weak to nucleate (w <= .63 at load); the collective, region-level A gives a global w(t) of 0.40-0.66 that reaches the pi requirement only at E0 = .6, marginally — consistent with ~0.7 but not a derivation. |
| Response time tau of the field | Paper: timescale separation (A2), no value. Eulerian field is worse (2.5–2.9). | tau must be a fraction of h/U, i.e. it scales with the flow, not a constant: tau = c h/U with c ~ 0.3 reproduces .02 here. In the continuum h -> 0 forces tau -> 0: the field must respond instantaneously to the load, which is a statement about the theory (theta as a constraint, like pressure) rather than a number. |
| Interface threshold | Paper: theta = pi (Dirichlet). The theory's 3 / pi / pi-3 structure should say which; at present 3 is the operational threshold and pi-3 = .1416 is the cushion by construction. |

The decision is what J *is*: interception (kappa <= 1) or an independent flux (unbounded, and then Ladyzhenskaya-type growth is what to demand). |

## The pre-registration these imply

For the external test, write down before running: weight w (0.7 recommended for the tube family), tau = 0.3 h/U, threshold at the wall onset 3, kappa = 1 (interception), beta_b = 0.5, floor -pi, gate a; predicted Gamma volume fraction 0.1–0.3%, predicted peak-enstrophy reduction 3–8% and peak-vorticity reduction 6–12% at Re ~ 300 on Kerr's initial data; predicted D > 0 on Gamma at all times. A result outside those ranges, or an interface that does not sit on the load, falsifies the form as specified.
