# Outside review of the navier_stokes_theta folder, 13 September 2026

Read: all *_RESULTS.md, FINDINGS.md, ORIGINAL_THETA.md, BROWSER_RECOVERY.md,
experiment.py, vortex3d.py, and the solver structure of helical_annulus.py /
sheath_chain.py. Not re-run.

## What is good

- Numerical hygiene is well above the usual standard for exploratory work:
  every run carries an energy budget residual, a CFL ceiling, at least two
  grids, usually a time-step halving, and an analytic tracker/solver check.
- The write-ups separate "what the forcing imposed" from "what the fluid did",
  and say plainly when a result is a response to the chosen forcing. That
  discipline is the main asset of the folder; keep it.
- The reduced models (U-tube oscillator, thermomagnetic loop, aperture transit)
  each isolate one mechanism and answer one question. Good decomposition.

## Where it has drifted

1. Theta has left the folder. ORIGINAL_THETA.md recovered the user-confirmed
   formula and listed four unresolved items (weights/powers, below-baseline
   domain, physical baselines, coupling law). None of the ~20 experiments since
   touches any of them. The only theta ever tested (P/D) was a new invention
   and it failed. The folder name promises a test that has not started.

2. "No singularity established" is true in every report, but for most runs it
   could not have been otherwise:
   - The 2D forced-shear experiment cannot blow up by theorem (2D global
     regularity is classical). FINDINGS.md should state that.
   - The 3D runs use nu = 0.03, U ~ 1-3, L ~ 1, so Re ~ 30-100. Dissipation
     rate ~0.036 gives a Kolmogorov scale eta ~ 0.17 and k_eta ~ 6 against a
     dealiased k_max ~ 10.7 at 32^3. So the runs are (marginally) resolved
     precisely because they are laminar. Nothing about regularity can be
     learned in this regime, resolved or not.
   - The "energy-calibrated controller" injects discrete velocity increments
     between RK4 steps. It is not a forcing in the sense any regularity
     statement uses (smooth or L^2-in-time). Good as an actuator surrogate,
     not as a candidate blow-up construction.
   - Peak SPEED is the headline diagnostic in the later reports. The
     regularity criterion is vorticity: Beale-Kato-Majda says blow-up at T
     requires int_0^T ||omega||_inf dt = infinity. Enstrophy growth rate and
     max |omega| should be the primary curves, speed secondary.

3. The engineering branch (pins, ferrofluid, apertures, planet orbit) is
   interesting but is now a separate project. Mixing it with a regularity
   question makes both weaker.

## What I would add

A. Finish the theta test that ORIGINAL_THETA.md set up. The pieces now exist
   in this folder: thermal_loop.py evolves T and viscous heating (so entropy
   production 2 nu S:S / T is available); the controller supplies a demanded
   power; ring_long reports the dissipation actually absorbed. Define
   theta(x,t) from the recovered formula with explicit, written-down choices
   for S_ref, T_ref, E_available, and the below-baseline rule (clamp, |.|, or
   signed root - pick one and say so). Then ask one question: does anything
   observable (hot-spot location, return-flow onset, gate arrival) correlate
   with theta crossing 1 / phi / sqrt(pi)? Still a diagnostic, but it is the
   test the folder was named for, and a negative answer would be informative.

B. If the real target is 3D blow-up, use the geometries the field already
   knows are the sharpest, and the diagnostics it uses:
   - Luo & Hou 2014 (PNAS): axisymmetric Euler WITH SWIRL in a cylinder,
     singularity forms at the wall. Chen & Hou 2022 turned this into a
     computer-assisted proof for Euler with boundary; Hou has since reported
     potential NS singularities with degenerate viscosity. This is very close
     to your "swirling column between counter-flowing regions" picture, and it
     tells you two things you are currently missing: the boundary matters, and
     the periodic box is the wrong domain for that scenario.
   - Lu & Doering 2008; Ayala & Protas 2017; Kang, Yun & Protas 2020: the
     initial conditions that maximize enstrophy growth in 3D NS are two
     colliding vortex rings / antiparallel tubes. Your "opposing tubes" and
     four-vortex runs are near this family. The right measurement is
     dOmega/dt versus Omega, compared with the analytic bound
     dOmega/dt <= C Omega^3 / nu^3, over a sweep in nu at resolution scaling
     like Re^(3/4). That converts "we saw a finite peak" into "we saw growth
     rate X% of the theoretical maximum".
   - Kerr 1993 antiparallel tubes remains the classical stress test.
   - Tao 2016 (JAMS) proved blow-up for an AVERAGED Navier-Stokes by building
     a self-replicating cascade "machine" out of the nonlinearity. Your chained
     stages and hand-off idea are that machine, built by hand. Tao's paper is
     also the clearest statement of why the true equation resists this: you
     cannot choose the coupling between scales, the bilinear term is fixed.
     Worth reading before adding more stages.

C. Housekeeping. An INDEX.md with one line per experiment (question asked,
   answer, status: established / suggestive / not tested) would make the
   folder legible. Right now the claim ledger lives only in a reader's head.

## Bottom line

Careful numerics, honest reporting, wrong regime and wrong diagnostic for the
question in the folder name. Either return to the recovered theta with the
pieces already built (A), or move to the known near-singular geometries and
enstrophy diagnostics (B). Both are doable with the present solver plus a
cylinder/no-slip variant.
