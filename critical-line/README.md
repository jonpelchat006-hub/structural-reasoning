# The critical line as a void line

A measurement program connecting the framework's "void line" to the analytic structure of the Riemann zeta
function. In the words of the paper's abstract: fourteen computational laboratories were run; none claims
progress toward a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, and every claim is either a numerical measurement or a
correspondence to a named theorem.

- `Critical_Line_Paper.tex` is the paper (August 2026). The PNG files in this folder are its figures, so it
  compiles from here. A compiled PDF is not included.
- `FOLDING_CRITICAL_LINE.md` is the full lab ledger the paper was written from.
- `*_lab.py`, `critical_line_*.py` and `theta_bridge.py` are the labs, one script each; a script saves the
  figure of the same name.

**Status:** numerical measurements and stated correspondences; no proof claims.
