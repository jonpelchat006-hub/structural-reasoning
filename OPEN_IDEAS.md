## The vesica as what the Q-tube rings assemble into

Jonathan, November 13 2026:

> the vesica piscis stuff might be what the qtube rings assemble into, and forming a larger ring with each
> of the smaller rings being for a knowledge domain. the vesica stuff we made early in the theory was above
> the ceiling in the folded bit picture. so i think this stuff was in the .14 band around 0. so weve been
> working inside the fold but then the vesica was above that.

The idea has two parts:

1. **Assembly.** The Q-tube's rings assemble into the vesica piscis construction from the early theory. They
   form one larger ring, and each smaller ring is a knowledge domain.
2. **Position.** In the folded-bit picture the early vesica work sits above the ceiling, in the .14 band
   around 0. The work since then has been inside the fold, and the vesica was above that.

**Status:** an idea. Nothing was built or measured for it.

### Handles for whoever picks it up

Added by Claude when the idea was written down. These are suggestions and are not part of the idea.

- **The number of domains fixes the geometry.** Two equal circles make a vesica piscis when each passes
  through the other's centre. Lay N equal rings of radius r flat around a larger ring of radius R. Neighbouring
  centres are 2R·sin(π/N) apart, so every neighbouring pair makes an exact vesica when R = r / (2·sin(π/N)).
  For three domains that is R = r/√3, for six it is R = r, and for twelve it is R ≈ 1.93 r. So the assembly
  half of the idea predicts one ratio between the domain rings and the ring they sit on. It also separates this
  arrangement from rings threaded on the large ring like beads, which never overlap and make no vesica.
- **The framework already says what the lens should hold.** In `SHOVELCAT_THEORY.md` memory is the overlap of
  two circles. If each domain is a ring of Q-tube nodes, the nodes two neighbouring domains share are the
  lens. A first test: knock out the shared nodes, then knock out the same number of unshared ones, and compare
  what each does to a task that needs both domains. The idea says the lens matters more.
- **The .14 means several things in these notes** (the last four rows of the table). The notes link them, but
  two of the links are marked as untested: `FOLDING_TRAMPOLINE.md` §9 holds the 0.14 seed as design and not as
  a result, and `FOLDING_HANDSHAKE.md` §5 says that identifying the reserve's close with the detector's
  measured residual is the untested step. The position half of the idea needs that settled first.
